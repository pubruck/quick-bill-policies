Privacy policy

This policy explains how Quick Bill, operated by S Jayarushanth, handles information in its Android restaurant point-of-sale app. Contact the administrator at pub.ruck@gmail.com.

Information used by the app

  • Account information: email address, Firebase account identifier, sign-in provider and any basic profile information supplied through Google sign-in. Email/password sign-in, where used, is handled by Firebase Authentication; the app does not store your plaintext password in its restaurant database.
  • Restaurant information: shop name, address, phone, menu items, prices, drink sizes/product codes, currency settings, purchases, bill numbers, timestamps, payment-method labels and recorded cash/change. Quick Bill does not process card payments or ask for card numbers.
  • Device and technical information: a device model label and a generated backup-copy identifier help identify cloud recovery copies. Firebase, Google sign-in and ML Kit may process identifiers, app/device information, network/IP information and operational/usage diagnostics needed to deliver and protect their services.
  • Local content: imported product photos, scan images, printer details and exported backups/reports. Product photos and printer preferences are retained locally or in backups you export rather than uploaded by the app’s cloud-journal synchronization.

How information is used

Information supports sign-in, administrator-controlled restaurant access, billing, receipts, business statistics, cloud backup/recovery, support, security and deletion requests. Account approval is separate from signing in. The current app has no advertising SDK or advertising-personalization feature.

Camera, photos and Bluetooth

Camera frames and selected scan photos are processed on your device by the bundled barcode/QR detector. The app does not upload these images or scan results to Google for barcode recognition. ML Kit can separately send operational and usage metrics to Google; see ML Kit privacy information. Bluetooth permissions connect portable receipt printers. On older Android versions, location permission supports Bluetooth discovery; Quick Bill does not use it to track your location. Clipboard text is read only when you tap a paste action.

Cloud services and disclosure

Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore, operated by Google, support authentication, access assignments and cloud backup/recovery. Google services may process information outside your country. See Firebase privacy information and Google’s privacy policy. We do not sell your personal information. Service providers process information for their services. We may disclose information when legally required or as necessary to protect accounts and prevent abuse.

Security and exports

Cloud connections use encrypted transport, and restaurant access is restricted through account approval and database rules. These protections are not a claim of end-to-end encryption. Local storage, Bluetooth printing and exported CSV/PDF/ZIP files have their own security properties. You choose where to save or share exports and are responsible for their handling.

Retention and your choices

Account and restaurant data remain available for the service until deleted or otherwise required for the stated purposes. Request deletion through Settings → Request account deletion or the public deletion page. We process verified requests within 30 days after account ownership is verified. We remove the account, access assignment and associated personal data; restaurant cloud copies are removed for a verified sole owner requesting their removal. Shared restaurant records require a rights review so another owner’s records are not erased. If a specific legal, security or accounting obligation requires retention, we will explain the affected categories, reason and applicable retention period before closing the request. Retained information is limited to that purpose.

Deleting a Quick Bill account does not delete your Google account. Exported files, paper receipts and copies on your own or another authorized device cannot be remotely erased by this request. Remove local app data and exported copies you control separately after preserving any records you must keep. Revoking camera/Bluetooth permission disables the corresponding scanning/printing feature.

Restaurant and customer data

Quick Bill is designed for business owners, not for children or customer delivery accounts. Walk-in sales do not require customer names or contact details. Do not enter unnecessary customer personal information in product names, notes or exported files. Restaurant owners remain responsible for information they choose to enter and for applicable recordkeeping obligations.

Contact and changes

Email pub.ruck@gmail.com for access, correction, deletion or privacy questions. Updates are published on this page with an updated date.
