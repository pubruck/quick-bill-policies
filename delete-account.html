You can request deletion without signing in here.

Delete your Quick Bill account

This page is for Quick Bill by S Jayarushanth. The administrator can verify and process your account and associated-data deletion request. The same option is available in the app under Settings → Request account deletion, and from the sign-in screen.

Send a request

  1. Use the email account you used to sign in to Quick Bill.
  2. Email pub.ruck@gmail.com with the subject Quick Bill account deletion request.
  3. State that you want your Quick Bill account and associated personal data deleted. Include the sign-in email and restaurant name. If available, include the account/restaurant references shown in the app’s prepared request.
  4. Tell us whether you are the sole restaurant owner and want its restaurant cloud data removed. Shared ownership is verified before restaurant records are erased.
Prepare deletion email

If your browser does not open an email app, copy the address and send the request from your usual mail service. Opening an email draft or this page does not submit a request. You must send the email. Never send passwords, verification codes or Google sign-in tokens.

Verification and timing

We verify that the requester controls the Quick Bill account. We may ask for a reply from the sign-in email or other appropriate ownership confirmation. Verified requests are processed within 30 days after ownership verification. No payment or unrelated action is required to submit a request.

What is deleted

The Quick Bill authentication account, its access assignment and associated personal data are removed after verification. For a verified sole owner requesting restaurant-data removal, the restaurant’s cloud records and device recovery copies are included. Shared restaurant records are reviewed to protect other authorized owners; personal account details are not retained simply because restaurant data is shared. We explain any specific legal/security/accounting retention requirement, affected categories and applicable duration before closing the request.

What stays under your control

Your Google account stays active. Files you exported, paper receipts and data on devices you or other owners control are not remotely deleted. Back up records you must keep before requesting deletion, and remove remaining local/exported copies separately. Uninstalling Quick Bill by itself does not delete its cloud account or cloud data.
